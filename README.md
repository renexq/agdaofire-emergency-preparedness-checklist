# 🔥 Agdao Fire & Emergency Preparedness Checklist

An educational website promoting fire safety awareness and emergency preparedness for residents of **Agdao, Davao City**, Philippines. It explains how fires commonly start in dense residential and market areas, and gives residents an interactive checklist to gauge — and improve — their household's readiness.

## 📖 About

Agdao's density is both its strength and its risk — tight rows of homes and stalls, shared walls, and overloaded electrical lines make it especially vulnerable to fire. This project was built to help residents understand **why** fires start in the barangay and give them a practical, actionable way to check their own preparedness.

It's a static, three-page website with no backend — just clean HTML/CSS/JS, deployed via GitHub Pages under a custom domain.

## ✨ Features

- **🏠 Home** — introduces the two leading local causes of fire (electrical overloads/illegal connections, and unattended cooking/candles/LPG leaks) with prevention tips for each.
- **🛡️ Preparedness** — dedicated page with fire and emergency preparedness guidance for households.
- **✅ Interactive Checklist** — a household readiness checklist with:
  - A live **readiness score / progress bar** that updates automatically as items are checked
  - The ability to **add your own custom checklist items**
  - A **Clear All** action to reset the list

## 🖱️ Usage

1. Open the **Home** page to learn how fires commonly start in Agdao and how to prevent them.
2. Visit **Preparedness** for broader household emergency-readiness guidance.
3. Go to the **Checklist** page and check off items as you complete them — your readiness percentage updates live.
4. Add any household-specific items with the **Add** field, or start over anytime with **Clear All**.

## ⚠️ In an Emergency

This site is for **educational and preparedness purposes only**. In an active fire or emergency in Agdao, Davao City, contact the **Bureau of Fire Protection** immediately.
