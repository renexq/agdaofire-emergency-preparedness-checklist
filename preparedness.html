Home Preparedness Checklist

Go-Bag Essentials

Pack one bag, keep it by the door, and check it every few months.

Essential

Flashlight & Batteries

For power outages and navigating dark, smoke-filled hallways.

Essential

Water, 1L per Person

Sealed bottled water, replaced every few months.

Essential

First Aid Kit

Bandages, antiseptic, burn gel, and daily prescription medicine.

Important

Documents, Waterproofed

IDs, titles, insurance — photographed and saved to the cloud too.

Important

Whistle

Three short blasts is the signal for distress if trapped or separated.

Backup

Cash & a Power Bank

Small bills for when card readers fail, plus a charged bank for your phone.
